About Orca Ventures

Built for complex operating environments.

Orca Ventures is a diversified company specializing in general construction, light-gauge steel, general trading, trade agency representation, and real estate services. Headquartered in the United States, we run regional offices in Saudi Arabia and Egypt — the Saudi office directs projects across the Gulf and Asia, and the Egypt office covers Africa, all under the guidance of our U.S. headquarters.

Mission

Exceptional services in general construction, general trading, real estate agency, trade agency, development, maintenance, and landscaping — with a focus on quality and sustainability.

Vision

To become a leading multi-service provider across the Middle East, Africa, and the United States, recognized for comprehensive, dependable delivery.
