Light Gauge Steel

Thin, lightweight galvanized steel members formed into C-sections, tracks and studs — a structural framing system built for precision, speed and long-term durability.

  • Lightweight Easier to handle and transport than heavier construction materials.
  • Strength ratio High strength relative to weight, without excess structural mass.
  • Durability Resistant to pests, mold and rot — lower long-term maintenance.
  • Sustainable Fully recyclable steel, reducing environmental impact.
  • Cost-effective Precise, faster construction cuts labor and overall cost.

A diversified operator in general construction, light-gauge steel, general trading & trade agency, and real estate development & brokerage — headquartered in the United States with offices in Egypt and Saudi Arabia.
